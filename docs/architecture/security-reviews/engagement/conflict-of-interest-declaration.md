## 2. Required disclosures

Disclose each actual, potential, or perceived conflict, or state “none identified” only after considering:

- authorship, design, implementation, operation, sale, or prior acceptance of the work being reviewed;

- compensation contingent on no findings, finding severity/count, acceptance, implementation sale, vendor choice, or project outcome;
- personal, family, supervisory, reporting, litigation/adverse-party, or close business relationships affecting independence;
- prior advice or opinion that the reviewer would be asked to validate;
- gifts, benefits, future-work expectations, or other incentives;
- subcontractor conflicts and evidence-access interests;
- legal/professional duties to another client or party that limit scope or disclosure.

## 3. Independence commitment

The declarant agrees to:

- update this declaration when team, scope, vendor interest, compensation, or relationships change;
- preserve independent findings and disclose limitations;
- not accept their own design/implementation without a separately approved independent closer;
- not condition findings or recommendation on product purchase or future work;
- identify factual input that comes from a conflicted source;
- pause affected work and notify the Owner if a new material conflict arises.
