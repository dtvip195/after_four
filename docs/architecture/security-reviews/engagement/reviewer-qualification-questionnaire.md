## 2. Expertise and experience

For each proposed person:

1. Describe directly relevant work, responsibilities, methods, and years/engagement count without including confidential client information.
2. Identify experience with the exact topics in scope, including failure/concurrency/operational evidence.
3. Provide permission-to-share redacted work samples, publications, talks, or equivalent artifacts.
4. Provide references that may be contacted and the permission/scope for verification.
5. List relevant education, training, licenses, admissions, and certifications with verification information. State how each relates to the scope; credentials alone are not sufficient.

## 3. Method and evidence discipline

- Describe review method and how it maps requirements to evidence, tests, findings, remediation, and acceptance.
- Explain how design proposals, implementation evidence, staging evidence, production facts, and professional judgment are distinguished.
- Describe positive, negative, boundary, concurrency, failure, and operational testing relevant to the assigned records.
- Explain severity/classification method and mapping to the AFTER FOUR model.
- Describe false-positive handling, disagreement resolution, retest, residual risk, and limitations.
- Identify evidence required at kickoff and evidence that cannot exist until implementation/staging.
- State how business-authority gaps will be kept separate from technical/legal conclusions.
