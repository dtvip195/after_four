## 3. Required analysis

- State jurisdiction, current sources, effective versions/dates, facts relied on, assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Separate conclusions into mandatory, prohibited, permitted with conditions, recommended, and unresolved/uncertain.
- Distinguish Owner-operational choices from non-variable legal requirements.
- Analyze data categories, purposes, minimization, notices, access/correction/other relevant rights, reader scope, employee/candidate/customer distinctions, recovery evidence, monitoring/session metadata, processors/providers, location/transfer, incident handling, retention/disposition, holds, exports, and anonymization as included.
- Identify additional business/provider/system facts required before a conclusion.
- Do not supply a universal numeric duration without explaining source, scope, triggering event, minimum/maximum character, exceptions, and conflicts.
- Identify re-review triggers such as law/guidance change, new provider/transfer, new data category/purpose, changed employment/customer flow, or incident.

## 4. Required deliverables

1. Dated engagement scope, reviewer identity/qualification, conflicts, sources, facts, and assumptions.
2. Topic-specific written opinion for each requested AF-PRIV record.
3. Requirement/recommendation table with source, applicability, responsible party, urgency, and uncertainty.
4. Proposed corrections to data/purpose/access/retention/hold/export/anonymization matrices without directly editing canonical policy.
5. Questions that remain for the Owner, technical team, operations, HR/finance, or providers.
6. Finding/action tracker and review of remediation documents.
7. Final recommendation per record, limitations, residual obligations, and re-review triggers.

## 5. Independence and confidentiality

Disclose provider/adverse-party/referral interests, prior advice being self-reviewed, subcontractors/local counsel, evidence locations/transfers, professional privilege/confidentiality assumptions, and data retention/destruction. Complete the shared [Qualification Questionnaire](reviewer-qualification-questionnaire.md) and [Conflict Declaration](conflict-of-interest-declaration.md). Accept or propose changes to [Evidence Handling Requirements](confidentiality-evidence-handling-requirements.md).

The Owner retains project approval authority but cannot convert project preference into a legal conclusion. Legal recommendation does not certify technical implementation or authorize Milestone 2B.
