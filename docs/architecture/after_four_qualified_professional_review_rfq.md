## 1. Project context and boundary

AFTER FOUR is preparing the business, data, authority, and internal-control design for an F&B operating model in Vietnam. Some current descriptions are Owner-confirmed desired processes for a First Branch that has not operated; they are not actual operating events or proof that a control works.

If a later Owner decision reactivates this contingency, AFTER FOUR could seek proposals from appropriately qualified independent professionals for the three separate packages below. A provider could propose for one or more packages, but would have to price and scope each package separately. A proposal would supply professional qualifications, approach, limitations, timing, and commercial terms; it could not decide Owner-controlled business policy or change repository governance status.

The [engagement governance document](after_four_identity_professional_review_engagement.md) would control provider qualification and due diligence, independence and conflicts, comparison and selection, Owner approvals, confidentiality and disclosure, commissioning and contract approval, work commencement, deliverable acceptance, and separation of any later independent readiness review. This RFQ is retained only as dormant contingency material; it defines neither an active procurement nor a current execution path.

## 2. Package A — CORE-01: Tax and Accounting Review

### 2.1 Objective and scope

Assess the qualified Vietnamese tax, accounting, and calculation questions needed before the Owner can decide the currently deferred commercial policies. The review must address:

1. Applicable tax requirements and legally required Product/Price display for the actual seller, First Branch, Commercial V1 Products and channels.
2. Accounting principles affecting calculation precision, representation, rounding, sequence, residual handling, correction, and later Refund allocation where applicable.
3. Open references `CORE-OD-03` and `CORE-OD-04` without treating either as already decided.
4. Applicable current Vietnamese law and professional standards, including effective dates, scope, assumptions, and uncertainty.
5. A clear separation among statutory requirements, accounting standards, professional recommendations, and business choices reserved to the Owner.

Commercial V1 selling-price inputs are a costing baseline, not final Price-publication or implementation authority. The review must not infer a Recipe, cost, margin, tax treatment, or rounding rule from that baseline.

### 2.2 Required deliverables

- A written Tax and Accounting Assessment.
- The applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, and technical basis, with dated citations and scope limitations.
- An issue/risk/recommendation matrix.
- Explicit assumptions, limitations, missing facts, and unresolved questions.
- Explicit decision points reserved to the Owner.

## 3. Package B — CORE-02 / D02: Identity, Evidence and Governance Review

### 3.1 Objective and scope

Assess the evidence, authority, identity, governance, privacy, and records questions needed to make the Owner-confirmed customer custody-transfer lifecycle reviewable without inventing operating evidence. The review must address:

1. Evidence classification and sufficiency.
2. Attester identity, authority, sufficiency, and separation.
3. Intended-customer and authorized-recipient linkage.
4. Missing, incomplete, unavailable, stale, ambiguous, conflicting, and disputed evidence.
5. Provenance, revision history, integrity, and traceability.
6. Privacy, purpose limitation, and access restrictions.
7. Non-destructive correction, retention, disposal, and legal hold.
8. Each open reference: `WS-C02-01`, `WS-C02-02`, `WS-C02-03`, `WS-C02-04`, `WS-C02-05`, `WS-C02-06`, and `WS-C02-07`.

The First Branch has not operated. The ordinary handover sequence is an Owner-attested `DESIRED_PROCESS`, not an `ACTUAL_EVENT`, documentary proof, or an approved evidence technology. Owner-selected Model A remains same-attempt, nonterminal, and fail-closed while evidence is unresolved. The proposal must not select an Identity technology, attester, resolver, evidence threshold, or customer consequence on AFTER FOUR's behalf.

### 3.2 Required deliverables

- A written Evidence and Governance Assessment.
- An evidence-class / attester / recipient-linkage / authority matrix.
- A risk and control-gap register.
- Recommendations, unsupported alternatives, assumptions, limitations, and explicit Owner decision points.

## 4. Package C — CORE-04 / D04: Internal Controls, Cash and Reporting Review

### 4.1 Objective and scope

Assess the internal-control, Accounting, Cash, Expense, Refund, Reporting, and records questions needed to complete the unresolved CORE-04 authority and control boundaries. The review must address:

1. Cash custody, counting, handover, Close, reconciliation, and discrepancy controls.
2. Operating Expense authorization and supporting evidence.
3. Refund request, approval, execution, and exception boundaries.
4. Segregation of duties, capability boundaries, delegation, revocation, and emergency handling.
5. Official-report issuance, read access, export, delivery, distribution, recipients, and purpose.
6. Linked correction and immutability of closed evidence.
7. Record retention, disposal, correction, and legal hold.
8. Nonzero Cash discrepancies, remaining detailed responsibility/control questions, Owner-unavailable cases outside the confirmed `WS-REFUND-01` disposition, and cross-midnight allocation.
9. Remaining CORE-04 decisions and control gaps identified by the current canonical worksheet and Owner Decision Baseline, including `CORE-OD-12`, `CORE-OD-13`, `CORE-OD-16B`, `CORE-OD-16F`, and the supporting `WS-C04-01` through `WS-C04-10` questions.

Confirmed policy includes zero discrepancy tolerance, reason and Owner notification for every nonzero discrepancy, Owner-only discrepancy disposition, mandatory Owner approval before every Operating Expense is incurred or executed, Owner-only Refund approval, Owner-only official-report issuance, and a `00:00 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` cutoff for the First Branch. Under `WS-REFUND-01`, an otherwise eligible Refund request received while the Owner is unreachable is recorded/acknowledged, evidence-preserved, pending and unexecuted, escalated to Owner, and communicated to the customer as pending; no automatic threshold, emergency delegate or implicit approval exists. Under `WS-OPS-01`, pending Employee Shift Handover keeps recorded custody explicit, permits an Owner-assigned named interim custodian, and otherwise pauses custody-changing operations under controlled hold without automatic role transfer or indefinite physical-presence obligation. These decisions do not establish Refund eligibility/method/execution/accounting treatment, Cash investigation handling, capability assignments, report readership/export/delivery, cross-midnight allocation, retention, or control effectiveness. The First Branch has no actual operating events from which effectiveness can be inferred.

### 4.2 Required deliverables

- A written Internal Controls Assessment.
- A risk / control / responsibility matrix that distinguishes confirmed authority from unresolved roles.
- A gap register, recommendations, assumptions, limitations, and unsupported alternatives.
- Explicit decision points reserved to the Owner.

## 5. Provider qualification and disclosure requirements

For every proposed package, the provider must disclose:

1. Legal entity, registration details, service qualifications, and contracting jurisdiction.
2. The accountable Engagement Partner or Director and the actual delivery team, including subcontractors.
3. Relevant professional qualifications, licenses, and the capacity in which each person will opine.
4. Demonstrated Vietnam-specific experience relevant to the proposed scope.
5. Relevant F&B, retail, accounting, internal-control, identity, governance, privacy, or records experience as applicable.
6. Proposed methodology and professional standards.
7. Independence, conflicts of interest, commercial relationships, referral interests, and prior work that could involve self-review.
8. Confidentiality, information-security, data-processing, storage, retention, return, and destruction practices.
9. Ability to report and provide clarifications in Vietnamese or bilingually.
10. Liability limitations, reliance restrictions, scope exclusions, and use of third parties.

These are proposal requirements, not findings that any provider has satisfied them. A title, employer brand, certification, or marketing statement does not by itself establish competence or independence.

## 7. Proposal evaluation framework

AFTER FOUR intends to compare proposals transparently against:

- Qualifications, licenses where applicable, and directly relevant completed work.
- Vietnam-specific legal, regulatory, accounting, governance, privacy, and professional capability appropriate to the package.
- Methodology, evidence quality, source discipline, treatment of assumptions, and ability to distinguish mandatory requirements from recommendations.
- Independence, conflict disclosure, subcontractor transparency, and protection against self-review.
- Confidentiality, information security, data processing, retention, return, and destruction controls.
- Deliverable clarity, source traceability, actionable risk treatment, and written clarification support.
- Feasible timeline, dependencies, team availability, and total cost.

No provider is selected, ranked, shortlisted, or approved by this document. Existing provider or market research is research only and is not qualification verification or selection.

## 9. Professional deliverable acceptance criteria

A deliverable can be considered for acceptance only when it:

1. Identifies the author, actual delivery team, accountable reviewer, and issue date.
2. Defines scope, facts relied on, assumptions, limitations, exclusions, and reliance restrictions.
3. Cites applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, professional, or technical sources with versions/effective dates where relevant.
4. Separates verified facts, Owner attestations, professional opinions, recommendations, and unresolved questions.
5. Provides actionable risks, control gaps, recommendations, dependencies, and residual uncertainty.
6. Explicitly reserves business-policy and authorization decisions to the Owner.
7. Can be clarified in writing by the accountable reviewer.
8. Meets the package-specific deliverables and acceptance criteria recorded in the canonical worksheet and engagement governance process.

Submission or acceptance of a professional deliverable does not itself record an Owner decision, close a canonical blocker, approve an implementation, or satisfy a later independent readiness review. Project acceptance, professional conclusion, Owner decision, and implementation authorization remain separate.
