## Decision status

- `OWNER_CONFIRMED`: the Owner explicitly approved the policy or scoped technical/validation direction stated in the row. Approval extends only to the row's stated boundary and consequence.
- `TECHNICAL_PROPOSAL`: recommended engineering default, not Owner approval.
- `SECURITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: control details that require security review before production.

Owner confirmation approves only the stated operating policy. It does not approve a hashing library, numeric PIN retry limit, MFA technology, cookie implementation, recovery mechanism, audit retention period, or other technical detail unless the row explicitly says so.

`WORKSHOP_AGREED` below is pre-existing domain provenance, not a Milestone 2A.1 decision status and not a substitute for Owner confirmation or security review.

## Remaining review and implementation decisions

- Enumerate ordinary and sensitive Branch Manager capabilities without expanding the business model; approve the step-up and MFA implementation.
- Define delegation race handling, invitation invalidation, and enforcement evidence.
- Approve authentication-lock release conditions and the Owner-unavailable escalation procedure.
- Complete the Owner-led security assessment of Owner recovery proofs, assurance, custodians, storage, rotation, delays, monitoring, and audit controls; independent verification remains not performed under ID-20.
- Select and review the production hosting, DNS, edge/proxy, private-connectivity, health-monitoring, and operational-ownership details without weakening ID-17.
- Complete legal/privacy review before assigning numeric audit-retention periods or finalizing visibility, holds, exports, recovery-evidence handling, and anonymization timing.
- Review session anomaly detection, new-device notification, device-summary privacy, and revocation behavior. No numeric cap is required for MVP.
- Execute and record Owner-led assessments under ID-20. Independent verification will remain not performed under the current approach, and legal/privacy uncertainty cannot be closed by Owner risk acceptance.
