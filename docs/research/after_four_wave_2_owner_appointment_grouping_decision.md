## 3. Shared-scope safeguards

### 3.1 Promotions scope

The shared Promotions coordinator scope covers three separately accountable Promotions requests. Shared coordination may reduce duplicated administration, but it does not merge the questions, evidence bundles, consulted-domain boundaries, later appointment decisions, or Owner outcomes. Catalogue, Voucher/privacy, and scheduling material must remain separately scoped and reviewed.

### 3.2 Inventory scope

The shared Inventory coordinator scope covers two Inventory-accountable requests without merging their domain responsibilities:

- Preparation-process evidence remains an attributable statement of actual workflow facts; it does not select Inventory recognition or valuation.
- Inventory/food-safety/accounting evidence remains separated into its applicable operational and professional scopes.
- A future proposed human must demonstrate suitability, access, confidentiality handling, capacity, and conflict clearance for **both** scopes. Suitability for one does not establish suitability for the other.

### 3.3 General safeguards

- Coordinator profiles are recommendations, not real identities, jobs, appointments, qualifications, or delegations.
- A shared coordinator never changes the accountable domain of any request.
- No person may self-verify organizational authority, qualification, suitability, conflict clearance, professional independence, disclosure scope, or evidence sufficiency.
- No coordinator may approve their own disclosure, recipient selection, communication channel, attachment bundle, or request delivery.
- `DEFER` and `REJECT_PROPOSED_APPOINTMENT` remain available for every later individual appointment decision.
- No later decision may record `APPOINT` or `APPOINT_WITH_SCOPE` without an Owner-supplied identity and verified bounded authority.
