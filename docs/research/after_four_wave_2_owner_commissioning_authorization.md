- actual recipient and organization match the authorization record;
- only approved attachments and versions are included;
- Customer/employee names, identifiers, contact details and transaction-level personal data are removed;
- credentials, keys, tokens, connection strings, internal URLs, security topology and exploit detail are removed;
- contracts, proprietary provider documents, prices, fees, recipes, formulas, payroll configurations and privileged advice are excluded unless separately authorized;
- examples are synthetic or irreversibly sanitized within the approved scope;
- document metadata, comments, tracked changes and hidden worksheets contain no excluded material;
- legal/professional source excerpts are necessary, current, and within retention/sharing rights;
- confidentiality marking, retention route and onward-sharing restriction match the approved channel; and
- a final attachment hash/version and disclosure record are retained before sending.
