## 2. How to read the ballot register

Each ballot's complete record is the pair of rows carrying the same stable ID in §§3 and 4. Section 3 supplies identity, decision, scope, exclusion, prerequisite, and responsibility fields. Section 4 supplies permitted/prohibited actions, destination, limits, dates, confidentiality, deliverables, acceptance, stop, downstream, and non-authorization fields. No row is executable until the Owner selects an allowed outcome and every recorded condition is satisfied.
