## 4. Cross-domain consistency findings

### 4.1 Payment, Refund, and cancellation

- `P1A-OD-03` confirms preparation of a method-aware, append-only Refund policy with authoritative reconciliation, cumulative successful-Refund cap, and actor/reason/approval evidence. It does not decide partial support, original/alternate method, line/component allocation, provider rules, or Cash effect.
- Before-Preparation and after-Preparation cancellation authority is confirmed; stage-specific financial, Preparation, Inventory, Loyalty, Promotion, remedy, and Reporting consequences remain deferred under `P1A-OD-04`.
- Payment Refund truth, Cash drawer movement, Loyalty reversal, Promotion reversal/release, and Reporting projection remain independent facts. No Refund amount automatically proves a Cash outflow or a proportional benefit reversal.
- Failed, rejected, cancelled, processing, and unknown Refund states appear as design evidence. Unknown outcomes require reconciliation; no executable lifecycle is authorized.

### 4.2 Catalog, Pricing, and Ordering

- `ODB-CAT-04`, `07`–`10` establish persisted Variant price input, Branch overrides, immutable effective Price versions, Draft repricing review, and confirmed sold-price snapshots.
- Tax, service charge, rounding, Modifier price effects, and zero/free-item behavior remain unresolved. No complete Order-total component vocabulary exists.
- Ordering owns the eventual calculation sequence and line/amount allocation; Catalog, Promotions, Loyalty, Payment, tax/accounting, and Reporting retain their input policies. `W2-INT-02A`–`02C` coordinate those inputs without creating shared ownership.
- Later Price or policy changes cannot rewrite confirmed sold facts. Corrections require linked provenance rather than historical mutation.

### 4.3 Loyalty and Promotions

- Loyalty earning is bounded to authoritative successfully settled eligible value; formula, eligible components, rate, rounding, and reversals remain open. Redemption requires explicit limits, ordering, and deterministic rounding before enablement.
- Promotions has confirmed directions for scoped applicability, explicit stacking/exclusion and priority, and distinct reserve/redeem/release/reverse outcomes. Benefit types, exact allocation, timing, and correction consequences remain unresolved.
- The one-Organization/one-Brand boundary and cross-Brand default deny remain controlling. No Organization-wide or cross-Brand Loyalty sharing or Promotion applicability is inferred.
- Voucher and initial Promotion type policy remain clarification items; exploratory concepts are not an approved type set.

### 4.4 Cash and financial evidence

- Operating Day uses an explicit Branch timezone/cutoff, but parameter values remain open. Cash Session, Employee Shift, Payment settlement, and Operating Day are separate concepts.
- Top-ups require Owner authorization; execution, fund-source classes, and accounting treatment remain conditional. Handover evidence is approved, while acknowledgement remains distinct from approval.
- Closed evidence is immutable; corrections are linked facts. Every nonzero discrepancy remains an exception requiring recording, reason, and Owner notification. Classification may drive investigation/escalation/resolution but cannot waive those obligations.
- A Cash Refund requires an approved Refund outcome and a separate Cash outflow fact. A non-cash or alternate-method label alone never proves drawer movement; only an approved Refund method outcome can identify the Cash effect.

### 4.5 Inventory and Preparation

- Normal consumption, waste, remake, and failed-Preparation deviations remain distinct. Deviation recording direction is confirmed; exact classes and authority remain conditional.
- Dispatch and receipt are separate custody facts. Stocktake adjustments require reason and capability-specific approval and cannot overwrite counts.
- Canonical units/versioned conversions are directionally confirmed; unit sets, ownership, version effects, and rounding remain open.
- `P2B-INV-OD-04A` has no selected Preparation recognition event. Neither Preparation start, completion, handoff, nor another event may be declared authoritative without Owner selection.
- Lot/expiry/quarantine/FEFO rules depend on item class and qualified food-safety evidence. Inventory valuation remains blocked on qualified Accounting.

### 4.6 Workforce

- Trusted time/device evidence and preservation of original/corrected facts are confirmed directionally; device/location rules, correction authority, and missed-punch handling remain conditional.
- Employee, User, Branch Assignment, Role/capability, Shift, Attendance, Leave, and Overtime remain distinct concepts.
- Breaks, grace, rounding, lateness, absence, leave, and overtime remain blocked on qualified employment/legal and named Payroll/Accounting/Reporting evidence. This repository supplies no legal rule, rate, entitlement, or threshold that can responsibly replace that evidence.

No confirmed cross-domain policy contradiction was found. The outstanding issues are incomplete policy, qualified-evidence absence, and maturity ambiguity—not incompatible approved directions.

## 9. Recommended consolidated Owner Review agenda

Use the fewest coherent sessions without mixing evidence acceptance with policy selection:

1. **Evidence Gate — five source-deficient candidates.** Review only the completeness and provenance of evidence prepared for the three clarification requests and two unresolved Conditional references. Parallel review is safe because none is a prerequisite for another within this group.
2. **Qualified Evidence Gate — three independent streams.** Review accepted employment/legal/Payroll evidence, Inventory accounting valuation evidence, and food-safety/item-class evidence. These streams may proceed in parallel and should not be combined into one policy decision.
3. **Commercial Policy Sessions.** After source evidence exists, close Refund/cancellation and Catalog commercial components in independent accountable-domain ballots; then review Loyalty, Promotions, and Cash downstream policies in parallel; review Ordering sequence last.
4. **Conditional residual-policy sessions.** Prepare separate bounded questions for unresolved parameters of the 22 confirmed directions. Do not ask the Owner to reselect the already-confirmed direction.

The fastest approach is parallel evidence preparation with sequential policy closure only where a real dependency exists. A single 39-item residual ballot would be faster administratively but would create a high risk of unsupported choices, accidental reopening, and cross-domain ownership leakage.

Candidates that must not appear on a substantive ballot yet are the three clarification references, two unresolved Conditional references, and all twelve Dependency-blocked references. Conditional residual parameters also remain off-ballot until their own option surfaces are complete.
