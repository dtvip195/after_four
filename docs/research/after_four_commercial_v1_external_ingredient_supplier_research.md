# AFTER FOUR — Commercial V1 External Ingredient & Supplier Research

**Research date:** 2026-11-24

**Scope:** Public external evidence for the 28 Owner-approved Commercial V1 Products and 52 priced Product/Variant combinations
**Status:** `EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_ONLY — NOT_ACTUAL_PURCHASE_COST — NOT_OWNER_APPROVED_BOM`

## 1. Executive summary

This review found usable public evidence from seven first-party ingredient-supplier sites: CloudMart, Hala Food, Kho Bách Phúc, Xuân Hà Food, Khởi Minh, Déli and Hân Hân Drinks. Two additional first-party packaging sources, Hunufa and Ly Giấy Việt Nam, were reviewed separately. CloudMart, Hala Food, Kho Bách Phúc, Xuân Hà Food and Hân Hân Drinks have a Da Nang or nearby first-party presence; Kho Bách Phúc explicitly states nationwide delivery and service to Central provinces, while Déli publishes a Da Nang-area branch and carrier/post delivery methods. None of the reviewed pages proves an AFTER FOUR-specific delivered price, stock reservation or delivery SLA to the first Branch in Hoi An.

The research captured 31 distinct publicly priced ingredient SKUs and four publicly priced packaging SKUs relevant to the candidate menu, plus five manufacturer recipe references. Those listings are useful for comparison and evidence-request preparation, but none is an invoice, accepted quotation or `ACTUAL_PURCHASE_COST`. No public source establishes AFTER FOUR's Recipe/BOM, serving quantity, yield, waste, approved substitute, packaging assignment, tax treatment or direct-cost allocation. Consequently, unit cost and gross margin remain uncalculable for all 28 Products.

All 28 Products have at least a candidate ingredient-category or supplier lead. Only fruit-based beverages, tea, cocoa/matcha and some baking inputs have useful public SKU evidence. Coffee beans, fresh fruit, dairy, yogurt, ham/cheese, bread, potatoes, frying oil and Product-specific packaging remain materially incomplete.

## 2. Method and evidence controls

- First-party supplier, manufacturer, product and recipe pages were preferred. Search snippets were not treated as evidence where a live page was available.
- All URLs were checked on 2026-11-24. A page's displayed price is recorded exactly; no supplier was contacted.
- Normalized unit prices are arithmetic comparisons only, computed only where the package quantity is explicit. They exclude delivery, discounts, tax adjustments, spoilage and conversion/yield loss.
- Stock status is recorded when visible. An out-of-stock listing remains market evidence, not procurement availability.
- Public recipe quantities are `REFERENCE_RECIPE`; they are not an AFTER FOUR Recipe/BOM and do not establish Product identity or commercial suitability.
- The canonical Commercial V1 prices, Products and Variants remain governed by [`after_four_real_menu_pricing_specification.md`](../architecture/after_four_real_menu_pricing_specification.md). This research does not amend them.

## 8. Ingredient, recipe and cost gaps

### 8.1 Shared ingredient gaps

- Coffee: approved bean/blend, roast, package, supplier, delivered price and extraction dose/yield.
- Fresh produce: lemon, kumquat, orange, passionfruit, avocado, young coconut, mango and strawberry grade, seasonality, purchase unit, delivered price and usable yield.
- Dairy: fresh milk, condensed milk, yogurt, cream and cheese identities, package, price, cold-chain and expiry loss.
- Staples: sugar, honey, soda water, salt, frying oil and ice/water direct-cost treatment.
- Food: bread, ham, cheese, potatoes/frozen fries and finished-versus-house-made bakery decision.

### 8.2 Recipe/BOM gaps

- All 52 priced combinations lack an approved ingredient line, recipe quantity and recipe unit.
- S/M/L scaling and cup fill/ice rules are absent.
- Batch recipes, batch yield and allocation are absent.
- Approved substitutes and substitution controls are absent.

### 8.3 Supplier-price gaps

- No accepted quotation, invoice or purchase order was found.
- VAT/invoice inclusion is generally unstated on listing pages. CloudMart is the documented exception: its general transaction terms state that website prices include VAT but exclude discounts, delivery and other costs; this still does not establish an AFTER FOUR landed price or invoice.
- MOQ, wholesale tiers, delivery charge, payment terms, validity and Hoi An landed price remain unknown.
- Promotional and out-of-stock prices require revalidation before evidence collection.

### 8.4 Yield, waste and packaging gaps

- No preparation yield or unavoidable waste rate is evidenced.
- No fresh-fruit usable-yield test is evidenced.
- No opening/expiry loss, batch carry-over or oil-use allocation is evidenced.
- No Product/Variant-to-cup/lid/container mapping exists; dine-in reusable serviceware and takeaway packaging remain separate missing evidence.

## 9. Hoi An procurement opportunities and constraints

1. **Best local comparison opportunity:** CloudMart plus at least one of Hala, Bách Phúc, Xuân Hà or Hân Hân can support a Da Nang-area shortlist without assuming national listings are locally economical.
2. **Best public price benchmark:** CloudMart and Khởi Minh publish the broadest exact package/price data. Khởi Minh is useful as a national comparison, not proof of Hoi An landed cost.
3. **Closest logistics lead:** Déli publishes a Điện Bàn Đông/Da Nang branch and carrier/post methods; no order, inquiry or disclosure is authorized by this report.
4. **Food/bakery lead:** Xuân Hà publishes relevant Rich's mixes and baking ingredients, but actual Product suitability and pricing require evidence not public on the reviewed pages.
5. **Main constraint:** no source combines approved recipe, current stock, accepted commercial price, tax, delivery to the first Branch and effective date. Those must be collected as separate evidence fields.

## 10. Recommended evidence-collection sequence

1. Owner/culinary input: freeze a Recipe/BOM candidate for each Product/Variant, including quantity, unit, batch yield, waste and approved substitute policy.
2. Packaging input: assign dine-in and takeaway packaging to each priced combination, including cup/lid/food container and per-order secondary packaging.
3. Quote preparation: use this report only to build a bounded SKU shortlist; request comparable delivered quotations only after separate authorization.
4. Evidence capture: record supplier, exact SKU/package, quote/invoice reference, price, tax statement, delivery, discount, evidence/effective dates and verification status in the canonical costing CSV.
5. Controlled calculation: calculate unit cost only when every required line has an evidenced recipe quantity, purchase-unit conversion, price, yield/waste and packaging allocation.
6. Commercial review: compare evidenced unit cost with V1 selling price; do not publish Price or claim margin until the unresolved governance boundaries are closed.

## 11. Limitations and governance boundaries

- No supplier was contacted; no quotation, sample, purchase, reservation or professional advice was obtained.
- Website prices can change and may exclude delivery, tax or quantity conditions. The access date is not a supplier price-validity commitment.
- Product-page claims were not independently laboratory-tested or authenticated.
- No external listing is an approved Ingredient, substitute, Recipe, direct-cost classification or procurement decision.
- No unit cost or gross margin is reliable for any Product/Variant at this stage.
- This report does not alter the 28 Products, 52 priced combinations, prices, channel scope, first-Branch/Price-Version scope, `CORE-OD-18`, `ODB-CAT-07`, tax/rounding decisions or any Gate/implementation status.
- No application code, schema, migration, seed, database, runtime configuration, external disclosure or contact authorization is created.
