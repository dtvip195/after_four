## 1. Executive result

All nine Wave 2 evidence requests have an accountable domain and documented recipient profiles, but none has a named human internal owner, identified actual recipient, verified recipient authority or qualification, approved request-specific scope, approved disclosure/channel, confirmed sharing right, or approved final request/attachment version. All nine therefore remain `NOT_READY_FOR_OWNER_AUTHORIZATION`, `PENDING`, and `NOT_SENT`.

Repository evidence resolves the responsibility shape, qualification criteria, minimum-disclosure controls, and sequencing of prerequisite work. It cannot resolve organization-specific appointments, actual provider/professional identities, engagement or contractual rights, request applicability, or Owner authorization. Those facts must be supplied and reviewed without sending a request.

The terms below are deliberately distinct:

- **Accountable domain:** owns the policy question and request outcome.
- **Responsible internal role:** prepares, scopes, or reviews evidence for that domain.
- **Named human owner:** an appointed person; none is identified in the repository.
- **External qualified professional/provider:** an actual identified and verified recipient; none is identified in the repository.
- **Owner authorization:** a separate decision permitting bounded delivery; none is recorded.

This assessment uses the [Owner Decision Baseline](../architecture/after_four_owner_decision_baseline.md), [Owner Commissioning Authorization](after_four_wave_2_owner_commissioning_authorization.md), [Owner Commissioning Review Session](after_four_wave_2_owner_commissioning_review_session.md), [Recipient Prerequisite Discovery](after_four_wave_2_recipient_prerequisite_discovery.md), [Evidence Acquisition Execution](after_four_wave_2_evidence_acquisition_execution.md), [Evidence Commissioning Package](after_four_wave_2_evidence_commissioning_package.md), and [Cross-Domain Evidence Synthesis](after_four_wave_2_cross_domain_evidence_synthesis.md).

Canonical policy and candidate status come only from the Owner Decision Baseline. Commissioning documents define draft questions, recipient profiles, acceptance controls, and delivery state; they do not constitute received evidence or Owner policy.

Prerequisites use exactly these classifications:

- `SATISFIED`: explicit repository evidence completes the prerequisite.
- `PARTIALLY_SATISFIED`: a framework, role profile, criterion, or draft exists, but the request-specific fact or approval is incomplete.
- `NOT_SATISFIED`: required evidence is absent.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: explicit evidence establishes that the prerequisite does not apply.

Absence was confirmed by repository search; a role title, document author, commit author, or accountable domain is not evidence of a human appointment.

## 6. Smallest safe next-action plan

Stages are sequential authorization boundaries; completing one does not authorize the next.

1. **A — Internal accountable-owner identification.** Owner/operator supplies an actual internal person and organizational authority for each request. This can proceed for all nine in parallel and does not contact a recipient.
2. **B — Owner confirmation of internal responsibility.** Where appointments are not already evidenced, the Owner confirms who may prepare and coordinate each request. Shared appointments may cover a bounded batch only when scope is explicit.
3. **C — External qualified-recipient identification.** Internal owners nominate actual organizations and people, then document qualification, engagement, provider authority and conflicts. The five professional/provider streams—Cash/accounting, Promotions/privacy/tax, Payment provider, Workforce/counsel/Payroll, Inventory/food-safety/accounting—can be investigated in parallel without sending requests.
4. **D — Scope, disclosure and confidentiality review.** Define each request's exact applicability facts; minimize/redact proposed content; verify privilege, contractual sharing, confidentiality and redistribution rights; choose an approved handling channel.
5. **E — Final request and attachment review.** Freeze exact request text, attachment manifests/hashes, source versions, recipient and channel. Re-run prerequisite classification against the frozen bundle.
6. **F — Separate Owner delivery authorization.** Present each request independently, or an explicitly bounded batch listing every request, recipient, scope, attachment and channel. Authorization to send is not evidence of sending.

Parallel preparation is safe for stages A–C and for non-disclosing draft scope inventories in stage D. No message, invitation, upload, observation, professional engagement, procurement, or disclosure may occur during this work. Requests can advance at different speeds; one completed record supplies no authority for another.
