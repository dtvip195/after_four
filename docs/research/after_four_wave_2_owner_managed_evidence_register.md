### 3.1 Reading the register

- **Owner decision** means a direction already recorded in the canonical Owner Decision Baseline. It is controlling within its exact boundary and is neither proved nor graded by vendor evidence.
- **POS evidence** means only the product fact or example actually supported by the cited vendor source. It must not be read as AFTER FOUR policy.
- **Remaining gap** means the detail established by neither the cited Owner decision nor the cited vendor evidence.
- Relationship labels (`DIRECT`, `INDIRECT`, `CONTEXT_ONLY`, `NO_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_FOUND`) follow the [comparative-evidence method](after_four_wave_2_vietnam_pos_comparative_evidence.md#2-method-and-evidence-grading). They grade only the cited external POS/vendor evidence for its stated subdimension; they never grade, validate, weaken, or strengthen an Owner decision.
- `SOURCE_IDENTIFIED` and `GAP_REMAINS` are working review labels, never replacements for canonical status.
- “Verified” is the date this repository row was checked, not an external publication or professional-validation date.
