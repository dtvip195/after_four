This session makes those six human-appointment questions independently answerable. It does not preselect a person or outcome.

The [Commissioning Prerequisite Resolution](after_four_wave_2_commissioning_prerequisite_resolution.md) controls request accountability and remaining prerequisites.

Available outcomes for each ballot are:

- `APPOINT`: appoint the Owner-supplied person for the complete verified scope stated in that ballot.
- `APPOINT_WITH_SCOPE`: appoint the Owner-supplied person only within explicit requests, entity/domain/Branch/process, period, access, and conditions.
- `DEFER`: record no appointment because identity or verification evidence is incomplete.
- `REJECT_PROPOSED_APPOINTMENT`: reject the proposed person/profile for that scope without appointing a replacement.

No outcome is selected below. A blank identity cannot support `APPOINT` or `APPOINT_WITH_SCOPE`.

| Owner outcome | Explicit unambiguous selection and any conditions for this ballot | `PENDING` |

The coordinator need not head the accountable domain. A properly authorized person may coordinate a bounded scope when all required evidence is independently verified.

## 5. Shared Promotions and Inventory safeguards

### 5.1 Promotions

- Catalogue, Voucher/privacy and scheduling retain separate scopes, source records, evidence bundles, consulted-domain boundaries, risks and later Owner outcomes.
- Suitability must cover all three streams or the Owner must use `APPOINT_WITH_SCOPE`, defer an uncovered stream, or reject the proposal.
- Coordination grants no Catalog, Ordering, Customer, Privacy, fraud/risk, Branch/Operations, legal, tax or accounting authority.

### 5.2 Inventory

- Preparation workflow facts remain distinct from Inventory recognition, food-safety applicability, valuation and accounting.
- The proposed human must independently demonstrate suitability and access for both Preparation-process coordination and Inventory-evidence coordination.
- Suitability for one scope does not prove suitability for the other. Food-safety, accounting, valuation or professional authority cannot be inferred from coordination.

Shared coordination changes neither accountable domains nor the requirement for independent verification and separate later delivery authorization.
