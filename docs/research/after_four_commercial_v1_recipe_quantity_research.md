# AFTER FOUR — Commercial V1 public recipe and quantity research

## 1. Purpose and authority boundary

This document records public recipe evidence for the 24 Owner-confirmed `MAKE_IN_HOUSE` Products in Commercial V1. It covers all 48 approved Product/Variant combinations (six one-size coffee drinks, twelve S/M/L drinks, four other one-size drinks, and two one-portion foods). It does **not** approve a Recipe, BOM, Ingredient, substitute, portion, yield, waste rate, packaging allocation, supplier or cost.

The cited formulations remain external reference points. No source establishes an AFTER FOUR serving size or a rule for scaling a source formulation to S/M/L. Every candidate formulation therefore requires Owner review and a practical test with the selected Ingredient specification, equipment, ice and serving vessel before it can enter a costing BOM.

### 1.1 Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning in this document |
| --- | --- |
| `SOURCE_VERIFIED` | The stated quantity/unit and method appear in the linked public source. This verifies the source statement only. |
| `RESEARCH_DERIVED_PROPOSAL` | A bounded candidate assembled from one or more source formulations; never an approved AFTER FOUR Recipe. |
| `UNVERIFIED` | The source omits the value, yield, conversion, or other fact needed to use the datum safely. |
| `OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | The Owner must choose or reject the candidate direction before it becomes policy or costing input. |
| `PRACTICAL_TEST_REQUIRED` | A controlled beverage/food test is needed to establish sensory result, actual output, loss and repeatability. |

No volume/mass conversion is made unless the source itself states both units. US cups, tablespoons and fluid ounces are retained as published.

“Comparable” does not mean interchangeable: formulations often use different ingredient forms, glass sizes and techniques. Manufacturer recipes are useful operational benchmarks but are promotional and ingredient-specific. General culinary sources are not Vietnamese café standards. Two URLs from one publisher, campaign, or shared formulation provenance count as one source family, not two independent sources.

Coverage types are distinct: **direct named-Product recipe** means the source identifies substantially the named drink/food; **related recipe/comparator** supplies a useful ratio or technique for a different formulation; **partial ingredient/method reference** supports only the stated component; and **insufficient quantity evidence** provides no usable Product portion. Every row in §4 states its limitation; the 24/24 figure includes direct recipes, related comparators, and partial references and therefore is not a count of complete café Recipes.

## 5. Cross-size, yield, waste and packaging findings

1. No source establishes an AFTER FOUR S/M/L cup capacity or scaling rule. A 500 ml source cannot be divided or multiplied into S/M/L without Owner-approved serving capacities and practical fill tests.
2. “One glass,” “one serving,” and source glass size are not interchangeable with an approved Variant. Ice displacement, garnish and foam make nominal vessel volume different from liquid yield.
3. Fruit counts (“one mango,” “three lemons,” “two–three kumquats”) are not costable purchase-to-preparation conversions. Actual incoming mass, trim, juice/pulp yield and rejected-fruit loss must be measured.
4. None of the sources establishes AFTER FOUR waste/remake assumptions, batch hold time, or Dine-in/Takeaway packaging allocation.
5. Manufacturer syrups/purées and brand-specific products are candidate Ingredient forms only. Their appearance in a recipe is not supplier or Ingredient approval.
6. Hot/iced choices for single-size drinks remain preparation choices rather than separately priced Variants under the Commercial V1 baseline; this research does not change that boundary.

## 7. Practical test plan inputs

For each Product/Variant, a controlled test record should capture: source formulation tested; exact Ingredient SKU/specification; weighed input; equipment and settings; water temperature/extraction time; ice mass; vessel capacity; output mass/volume; preparation loss; sensory result; Dine-in and Takeaway presentation; repeat count; tester; date; and Owner disposition. Conflicting formulations should be tested as separate candidates, never averaged silently.

Priority sequence:

1. Proceed with bounded practical-test planning for Cà phê muối (salt/foam/extraction) and Trà đào cam sả (tea/citrus/lemongrass balance). Bơ xay dừa non remains a research task: establish the young-coconut Ingredient form/specification and intended Product characteristics first. It cannot enter practical Recipe testing until those prerequisites are sufficiently defined and separately reviewed.
2. Lock reusable bases only after tests: phin coffee, tea base, lemonade/citrus base, smoothie liquid base and packaging fill standards.
3. Test S/M/L independently after vessel capacities are approved; do not assume linear flavour or ice scaling.
4. Record actual yield and loss before using any recipe in costing.
