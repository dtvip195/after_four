## 1. Purpose and authority boundary

This package converts the evidence gaps retained in the [Wave 2 Evidence Closure Readiness assessment](after_four_wave_2_evidence_closure_readiness.md) into bounded requests for accountable business stakeholders and qualified reviewers. It commissions evidence; it does not obtain, accept, or interpret a future response in advance.

The [Owner Decision Baseline](../architecture/after_four_owner_decision_baseline.md#wave-2-conditional-direction-and-clarification-responses) remains canonical. A request owner coordinates an evidence response but receives no delegated Owner authority. A qualified reviewer supplies attributable interpretation within their competence but does not select AFTER FOUR policy. Only a later, separately governed review may determine that a gap is closed and prepare an Owner ballot.

## 4. Five evidence commissions

### 4.1 Commission EC-W2-01 — `P2B-CASH-OD-03` Cash discrepancy

**Unresolved question:** Within the approved no-tolerance and immutable-close baseline, how are nonzero discrepancies classified, investigated, escalated, corrected through linked facts, accounted for, reconciled, and treated in authoritative Reporting, and who may initiate, review, and approve each permitted action?

**Why current evidence is insufficient:** Current evidence requires recording, reason, and Owner notification for every nonzero discrepancy. It does not supply defensible discrepancy classes, linked-correction operations, authority choices, journal consequences, or Reporting hold/release outcomes. Notification is not approval. `ODB-CASH-14` confirms linked correction facts without reopening or rewriting closed evidence; it does not authorize any specific post-close correction operation or define its initiator, reviewer, or approver.

| Commission element | Required response |
|---|---|
| Questions for Cash operations | What actual discrepancy situations occur? Which investigation and linked-correction actions are operationally meaningful? What evidence exists before/after each action? How are disagreement, overdue investigation, and unresolved cases handled today? |
| Questions for the qualified accountant | For each proposed action and unresolved outcome, what accounting treatment and journal implications apply to the identified AFTER FOUR entity/regime? Which source versions, assumptions, materiality concepts, period-close constraints, and limitations apply? |
| Questions for Reporting | When may a report remain authoritative, become qualified, be held, or be released? How must an unresolved discrepancy and later linked correction remain visible? |
| Questions for authority design | For each candidate operation, which business responsibility may initiate, independently review, approve, reject, or escalate it? What separation is required, and what evidence supports that boundary? |
| Acceptable artifacts | Signed/attributable accounting advice; approved Cash operating procedure or actual workflow record; Reporting treatment matrix; role-responsibility matrix; traceable primary legal/accounting sources attached to the professional interpretation |
| Provenance required | Author/reviewer identity and role; professional qualification where applicable; organization; entity/regime and Reporting scope; assessment date/timezone; source versions; assumptions, exclusions, conflicts, and review trigger |

**Must remain undecided:** discrepancy classes, materiality consequences, correction operation set, initiator/reviewer/approver assignments, journal treatment, Reporting hold/release, schema, workflow, and enforcement. No tolerance or threshold may be introduced through this commission.

**Gap-closure criteria:** every proposed ballot alternative maps to actual Cash cases; preserves recording, reason, Owner notification, immutable closed evidence, and linked corrections; identifies authority and separation; contains attributable accounting and Reporting consequences; covers corrected and unresolved outcomes; and states material conflicts or limitations. Cash confirms operational accuracy, qualified Accounting confirms its propositions, Reporting confirms report consequences, and a governance reviewer confirms provenance. This sufficiency review selects no policy.

## 7. Evidence acceptance and provenance standard

An evidence response is reviewable only when it contains:

1. a stable commission and candidate reference;
2. author/reviewer identity, role, organization, and relevant qualification;
3. assessment date and timezone;
4. applicable entity, jurisdiction, location, product, employee class, provider, workflow, channel, and period, as relevant;
5. primary legal/regulatory sources and their current amendment, effective-date, and transition status for legal propositions;
6. source/document versions and immutable artifact references or retained signed output;
7. facts separated from professional interpretation, business recommendation, unresolved assumptions, and Owner policy choice;
8. explicit assumptions, exclusions, conflicts, uncertainty, residual risk, and review/expiry trigger; and
9. answers to every commission question or an explicit `NOT_ESTABLISHED` response with the reason.

Qualified Vietnamese legal, tax, accounting, employment, privacy, or food-safety advice is required where applicability or professional interpretation is material. A statutory link alone is not professional advice. General statutory text must not be converted into a specific AFTER FOUR rule without attributable applicability review.

Operational propositions require actual approved procedures, observed workflow evidence, provider documentation, or traceable source records. Preparation and Branch facts cannot be invented from UI state. Payment behavior requires provider-specific documentation. Secondary/explanatory sources may aid navigation but cannot replace controlling primary sources or scoped professional interpretation.

## 8. Proposed commissioning workflow and responsibility profiles

The following is a documentation workflow proposal, not an application state machine or approved business policy:

```text
DRAFT → REQUESTED → EVIDENCE_RECEIVED → REVIEWED → GAP_CLOSED / GAP_REMAINS
```

| Stage | Documentation meaning | Responsible role | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DRAFT` | Request checked against canonical scope and questions | Accountable domain request owner + governance reviewer | Complete commission draft; no policy claim |
| `REQUESTED` | Bounded request delivered to the identified role/provider | Request owner | Dated request, recipient role, accepted scope; no invented deadline or commitment |
| `EVIDENCE_RECEIVED` | Response and attachments retained without acceptance inference | Request owner / evidence custodian | Immutable artifact reference, provenance fields, response date, limitations |
| `REVIEWED` | Scope, currency, competence, conflicts, contradictions and completeness assessed | Accountable domain; qualified peer where needed; governance reviewer | Review record distinguishing facts, interpretations, recommendations and gaps |
| `GAP_CLOSED` | All closure criteria met sufficiently to prepare a later Owner ballot | Accountable domain confirms business completeness; qualified reviewer confirms scoped professional propositions; governance reviewer confirms provenance | Closure finding with evidence map; still no Owner decision |
| `GAP_REMAINS` | Missing, conflicting, stale, out-of-scope or non-attributable evidence persists | Same reviewers | Exact deficiency, responsible provider and next bounded action |

**Qualified reviewer profiles:** Vietnamese counsel in the relevant legal field; qualified Vietnamese accountant/tax adviser for the applicable entity/regime; Payroll/provider subject-matter owner; privacy counsel; food-safety adviser for the actual item/location/regulator; Payment provider/acquirer representative; or accountable operational process owner with direct workflow knowledge. No firm or individual is selected here.

**Independence/conflicts:** responses must disclose whether the reviewer designed, sells, operates, audits, approves, or benefits from the proposed option. Where professional independence or separation is material, the review record must identify the concern and whether independent review is required. This package does not define a legal independence standard.

**Escalation when evidence is unavailable:** record `GAP_REMAINS`, the attempted source/provider role, the missing proposition and its decision impact. Escalate to the accountable domain and Owner for a choice to defer, narrow the question, commission another qualified source, or explicitly accept a later documented uncertainty. Do not substitute generic research or silently relax closure criteria.

## 10. Evidence closure criteria and next review trigger

A commission may trigger a new readiness review only when:

- every required question has an attributable answer or explicit unresolved finding;
- every artifact satisfies §7 provenance and currency requirements;
- the accountable domain confirms factual/business-surface completeness;
- required qualified reviewers confirm only their scoped professional propositions;
- cross-domain owners confirm their authoritative inputs without transferring policy ownership;
- contradictions and minority/alternative views are retained rather than normalized away;
- the candidate-specific closure criteria in §4 are met; and
- a governance reviewer verifies that no evidence response has been misreported as an Owner decision, Gate, or implementation authorization.

The next review must state whether the retained evidence is sufficient for later ballot preparation, whether an evidence gap remains, or whether an upstream dependency remains. That assessment must not overwrite canonical candidate status. Owner ballot preparation, Owner selection, canonical recording, Gate review, and implementation authorization remain later, separate actions.
